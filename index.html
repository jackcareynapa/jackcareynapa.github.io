Wikinaut

Userscript + Flask · Tampermonkey / Fly.io

Chart the shortest link-path between two Wikipedia articles, then fly it hop by hop, clicking the real links on real pages. A Tampermonkey userscript drives the flight and matches links in the DOM; a Flask backend runs bidirectional BFS over the full English Wikipedia link graph with memory-mapped CSR arrays.
